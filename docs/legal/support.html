What Rally is

Rally is a weekly goal app you use with a few people you actually know.

At the start of the week you stake a small number of goals — a handful, not a list of forty. Each one is worth points. You can pair with someone in your circle on a goal, so you are both on the hook for it. Through the week, people cheer each other's goals as they close. On Monday the week rolls over, the points are totted up, and you start again.

You choose who sees each goal: your circle, everyone on Rally, or nobody but you. A circle is a small private group you create and invite people into with a code.

Getting help

There is no help desk and no ticket system. Email lordruckus.nb@gmail.com and describe what happened. It helps a lot if you include:

  • Your display name in the app, and the name of your circle if you are in one;
  • Your phone and OS version (iPhone 15, iOS 18.2 — that sort of thing);
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • A screenshot, if there is anything to see.

If the app showed you an error screen with a block of text on it, a photo of that screen is the single most useful thing you can send.

Reporting content or a person

If someone posts something abusive, or you see a goal, a note or a profile that should not be there, there are two things to do, and they do different jobs.

1. Report it in the app

Open the goal, note or profile, and choose Report. You will be asked to pick a reason: harassment or bullying, spam, sexual content, violence or threats, self-harm, or something else.

Reporting does two things immediately: it hides that content from your own feed on that device, and it records the report on our servers. The person you reported is never told, and cannot find out that you reported them.

Be aware of the limits, because we would rather tell you than have you assume: the in-app report is a record, not an alarm. Hiding is local to the device you reported from, so the same account on another phone will still see the content until you report it there too.

2. Email us if it needs someone to act

For anything that needs content taken down, an account dealt with, or a human decision of any kind, email lordruckus.nb@gmail.com. This is the route that reaches a person, and it is the one to use if you have already filed an in-app report and nothing has happened.

Tell us what you saw, where (a goal, a note, a profile), and the display name of the person involved. We will look at it, remove content that breaks the rules, and suspend or delete accounts that keep doing it. Expect a reply within 24 hours, and sooner for anything marked urgent.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services. We are a small app and we cannot act quickly enough to be the right first call.

Blocking someone

Blocking is the immediate, self-service option, and it does not need us. Open the person's profile and choose Block. From that moment you stop seeing their goals, notes and cheers, and they stop seeing yours — it works in both directions. Blocking follows your account, so it holds on every device you sign in on.

Blocked people are listed under Settings, at the foot of the Me screen, where you can unblock them.

Two things worth knowing. Nothing in the app announces a block, but a person paying attention may work out what has happened, since their cheers stop landing. And blocking someone in your circle does not remove their scores from the circle's weekly totals — it hides what they say, not the arithmetic the circle shares. If you want out of that entirely, leave the circle.

Deleting your account and your data

Open Me, then Settings, and scroll to Delete my account at the bottom. The screen there lists exactly what goes and what does not before you confirm anything.

You have fourteen days to change your mind. The moment you confirm, your account stops being visible to anyone else — you are gone from your circle and nothing you have written can be read. Fourteen days later it is destroyed and cannot be recovered by us or by anyone.

To take it back inside that fortnight, open the app and tap Keep my account on the first screen. On iPhone, signing back in with Apple does the same thing. After the fourteen days there is nothing left to bring back.

If you cannot reach that screen — the app will not open, or you have already deleted it from your phone — email lordruckus.nb@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account. Rally accounts have no email address attached, so tell us your display name, your circle's name or invite code if you are in one, and whether you secured the account with Sign in with Apple. Expect a reply within 24 hours.

Deleting the app is not the same thing. It clears everything stored on your phone, but your account and its contents stay on our servers. On Android, deleting the app also leaves you no way back in — so if you want the account gone, email us before you uninstall.

Deletion removes your profile, name and photo, every goal and photo you posted, every note you wrote or received, your cheers, your weekly totals, your notifications, your push tokens, your blocks, your circle memberships, and the notifications your cheers left in other people's apps. Three things it does not reach: reports other people have filed about you, because deleting an account cannot erase a safety record; a circle you created, which outlives you so the people still in it are not thrown out of it; and the anonymous cache of goal text used to price goals, which holds no link to any account. The privacy policy explains all three.

Want a copy of your data instead? Same address, subject Export my data.

Common things

I deleted the app and lost everything

On iPhone, if you had tapped Secure this account before uninstalling, choose Continue with Apple on the welcome screen and your account comes back — name, circle, current week, notes, cheers and closed weeks. If you had not, the account cannot be recovered, because nothing but that install held the key to it.

On Android there is currently no way to secure an account and no way to recover one. This is a real gap and we are working on it. Until then, treat an Android install as the only copy.

Recovery brought my totals back but not my old goal titles

Expected. A restored week brings back the numbers — points, goals done, whether the week was perfect — but not the list of what you did. Only the current week's titles come back.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Rally in your phone's settings, and that Settings in the app shows permission granted. Push notifications do not work in a simulator, only on a real phone. If the app is showing cheers when you open it but nothing arrives on your lock screen, that is worth emailing us about.

My photo was rejected

Every profile picture and goal photo is checked automatically before anyone can see it. If the check declines it, or cannot complete, the photo is not published and you are asked for a different one. If you think a perfectly ordinary photo is being rejected, email us — that is a bug worth knowing about, and it happens.

A goal I typed was refused

Goals are screened for content describing serious harm. The check is automatic and it is not perfect. If a reasonable goal was refused, email us and tell us what you typed; it helps us fix it.

Points for the same goal came out differently

Goal prices are assigned by a model reading the text, and it is not perfectly consistent — the same goal reworded can be worth a step more or less. Once a particular wording has been priced, that price sticks for everyone.

The app says something hasn't been sent

Rally works offline and sends things when it can. If a banner says something is unsent, leave the app open with a connection for a moment. If it stays that way for more than a few minutes, email us.

Privacy

What Rally collects, who it goes to, and how to get rid of it is set out in full in the privacy policy. The short version: no email address required, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking — but the goals you type and the photos you upload are sent to Google's Gemini API to be priced and screened.
