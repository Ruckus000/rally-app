Start with **[`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md)**. It is the plan: situation analysis,
the honest assessment, positioning, App Store strategy, go-to-market phases,
channel strategy with expected yields, growth-loop math, lifecycle design,
measurement, budget at $50 and what $500 and $5,000 would change, product
asks, the calendar, and risks.
