One post a week is the rule; more only when there is a number or a story.
Posts go on whichever network you already use (X, Threads or Bluesky) and are
cross-posted to iosdev.space when they are about the build. Every post ends
with the App Store link once it exists. Bracketed values come from
`metrics.sql` query 9 on the Monday morning; never round up.

Images: a ledger screenshot (with permission) beats any graphic. When there
is no screenshot, a single line in Bricolage 800 on ink with the lime mark,
1080×1350.

## What not to post

- Anything with "🚀" or "excited to announce."
- A number that isn't from the query.
- A screenshot of someone's ledger without asking.
- The Global feed. The people on it are fictional.
- More than one post a day. The app is about a week; the posting should be too.
