Every Monday, put a few goals on the line. Your circle sees them, cheers when you close one, and Sunday the week closes and asks what carries. No streaks. Nothing resets.

Get it on Google Play

Android today. iPhone when the developer program is paid for, which is soon. If your circle is on iPhone, say so and you'll be first to know.

How a week goes

  1. Monday: stake.Three or four things. Run twice. Ship the thing. In bed by eleven. Each is worth points by how hard it is.
  2. All week: cheer.Close one and your circle sees it. A cheer lands on your phone with their name on it.
  3. Sunday: the ledger.What you did, who helped you, who you helped. Then it asks what carries into next week.

What it isn't

  • Not a streak counter. A missed day costs you the day, not your history.
  • Not a public feed. Your circle is people you invited, by code.
  • Not a money app. The stake is your word, and the people who heard it.
  • Not watching you. No ads, no analytics, no email, no phone number. Delete your account from inside the app.
