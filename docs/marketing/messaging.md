# Messaging framework

Everything Rally says in public derives from this page. The customer phrases
come from `research/voice-of-customer.md`; the proof points come from the
code. The voice rules are the handoff's: plain, warm, second person, slightly
blunt; stakes language, not productivity language; never guilt-trip; never
a bare zero.

## Positioning statement

For small groups of friends who already talk about their goals and want the
talk to count, Rally is the weekly accountability circle that turns "I'll do
it this week" into something your friends can see, cheer, and hold you to.
Unlike money-stakes apps and streak trackers, Rally stakes a week, not a day,
with people you know, not strangers or fines. Free, no ads, no analytics.

## Three pillars

### 1. A week, not a streak

*Customer phrase:* "I had an 800+ day streak and I remember that on the days I
couldn't…"; "when life interrupts the streak the whole thing falls over."

*Claim:* Monday you stake a few things. Sunday the week closes and asks what
carries. A missed day costs you the day, not your history.

*Proof:* week rollover is built on the ISO week; the year grid records each
week as nothing, partial, good, or perfect; a week holds the streak bar if at
least one stake closed; nothing is ever reset for you.

### 2. Friends, not strangers or fines

*Customer phrase:* "an accountability service … with an actual human being";
"when everyone is posting goals, nobody is really watching yours"; "knowing
someone else was waiting for me."

*Claim:* Your circle is people you invited, by code. When you close a goal
they cheer, and it lands on their phone with your name on it. Pair up on the
hard ones.

*Proof:* invite-code circles with no public discovery; cheers reach the other
person's lock screen (remote push); "in it with me" pairing; ranking by
follow-through, not points; the weekly ledger names who helped you and who
you helped.

### 3. Nothing to sell you, nothing watching you

*Customer phrase:* "one more tab you'll forget to open"; every billing-surprise
review in the category.

*Claim:* No subscription, no ads, no analytics, no email. Delete your account
from inside the app.

*Proof:* no in-app purchases; the privacy policy names exactly two external
hosts; anonymous sign-in with optional Sign in with Apple; in-app deletion
completes in fourteen days.

## What Rally never says

- "Global community," "everyone," "thousands of users." The Global feed is
  fictional demo characters.
- "Habit tracker." It invites the comparison Rally loses on search and
  isn't the product.
- "Never miss a day," "keep your streak," "don't break the chain."
- "Crush," "grind," "level up," "productivity." Stakes language only.
- Any number that has been rounded up.

## Objection handling

| They say | Rally says |
|---|---|
| "People will just mark things done." | They can. Their circle is three people who know them, not a referee. Lying to your friends about a run is a different act from lying to an app, and most people won't. |
| "Without money on the line there are no consequences." | The consequence is Sunday. Your circle sees the ledger with your name on the row that didn't close. That's the consequence people told us they actually feel; a fine is the one they told us they avoid. |
| "Friends are too soft." | That's why it's a stake, not a chat. You wrote it down on Monday. Sunday the week asks. The app does the pushing so your friends don't have to. |
| "It's another app I'll forget to open." | One Monday reminder with your points on it. Cheers come to you; you don't go looking. Sunday closes the week whether you open it or not. |
| "Why not just a group chat?" | The group chat forgets. Rally remembers what you said on Monday, shows it to the same people on Sunday, and keeps a ledger of who showed up for whom. The chat is where you talk about it. Rally is where it counts. |
| "No streaks means no motivation." | Streaks motivate until the day they don't, and then people quit for two years. A week is long enough to recover a bad Tuesday and short enough that Sunday always comes. |
| "Too embarrassing to show friends my failures." | Choose who sees each stake: friends, everyone, or private. Nothing is public by default and nothing leaves your circle. |
| "iPhone only?" | For now. Say so and you'll be first to know. |

## Copy bank

**Store, first line:** Rally is for the goals you would actually keep if
someone were watching.

**Landing hero:** Stake your week. Your friends keep you honest.

**Landing sub:** Every Monday, put a few goals on the line. Your circle sees
them, cheers when you close one, and Sunday the week closes and asks what
carries. No streaks. Nothing resets.

**Invite (share sheet):** Join {circle} on Rally: {link}

**Perfect-week card, external:** {name} closed every stake this week. Circle:
{code}.

**Monday push:** Week {n} opens today. You staked {pts} pts.

**Sunday push (proposed):** The week closes tonight. {done} of {total} closed.
Sunday's the day.

**Thursday push (proposed):** Half the week left. The circle can't cheer what
it can't see.

**Build-in-public frame:** Week {n} of Rally in the wild. Circles: {c}.
People: {p}. Stakes: {s}. Closed: {d}. Cheers: {ch}. Perfect weeks: {pw}.
Learned: {one sentence}. Next: {one sentence}.

**Sign-off:** The week doesn't count itself.
